BUSINESS ECONOMICS
Acquisition Asymmetry vs. Liquidity Runoff
Institutional Focus: Basel III Capital Coverage
The Customer Acquisition Penalty
5x to 7x Cost Multiple
• In Western European banking, replacing a departing depositor requires €400–€600 in marketing and onboarding expense.
• New retail accounts take 18–24 months of steady transactional velocity just to amortize customer acquisition costs (CAC).
• Open banking protocols and automated switching services under PSD2 have completely eroded traditional procedural switching barriers.
Balance Sheet & Liquidity Impact
Basel III Core Deposit Flight
• Retail deposits represent the cheapest, most stable tier of capital under Basel III Liquidity Coverage Ratios (LCR).
• When affluent customers churn, core deposits vanish, forcing treasury teams to tap volatile wholesale repo borrowings at 350+ bps higher spreads.
• Retention is fundamentally a treasury solvency and net interest margin preservation strategy.

BEHAVIORAL SIGNALS
Engagement Signals: Active vs. Inactive Accounts
Metric: IsActiveMember Indicator
Inactive Accounts (IsActiveMember = 0)
26.85%
• Inactive depositors are 88% more likely to defect than active peers.
• Account dormancy is the most powerful behavioral precursor to account closure.
• Depositors typically stop logging in or swiping cards 60 to 90 days before formally switching banks.
Active Accounts (IsActiveMember = 1)
14.27%
• Digital engagement creates a powerful behavioral shield against competitor acquisition.
• Features like automated bill pay, daily notifications, and mobile app interactions drastically reduce churn.
• Early warning rule: Trigger proactive customer outreach upon 30 days of zero login activity.
