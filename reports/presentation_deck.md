## Slide 5: The Demographic Trap — The 46–60 Age Gradient

### Visual Layout

  - 30–45: **14.83%**
  - 46–60: **56.21%** (Peak Risk)

### Slide Content
- **Inverted U-Curve:** Churn is lowest among young digital natives (7.52%) and peaks dramatically in mature adults.
- **The 56% Cliff:** More than **one out of every two customers** aged 46 to 60 exited the bank.
- **Life-Cycle Mismatch:** Clients in this bracket are paying off mortgages and consolidating pre-retirement wealth. Our standard retail checking products fail to provide the wealth advisory capabilities they demand.

### Presenter Notes
> "Many assume young, tech-savvy customers under 30 are the most fickle. The data proves the exact opposite: under 30 is our stickiest segment at 7.5%. The crisis is in the 46 to 60 bracket, where churn reaches an astonishing 56%. These are our peak wealth-accumulators, and we are treating them with transactional retail accounts rather than private wealth advisory."

## Slide 6: Product Bundling Hazard — The Multi-Product Paradox

### Visual Layout
- Step-wise hazard curve chart:
  - 1 Product: **27.71%** Churn
  - 2 Products: **7.58%** Churn (Optimal Anchor)
  - 3 Products: **82.71%** Churn (Hazard Zone)
  - 4 Products: **100.00%** Churn (Total Defection)

### Slide Content
- **The Golden Anchor:** Holding exactly 2 products anchors the client with the lowest churn across the institution (7.58%).
- **The Cross-Selling Failure:** Holding 3 products spikes churn to 82.7%, and holding 4 products results in a **100% exit rate**.
- **Root Cause:** Pushing redundant, fee-heavy products onto customers creates billing friction, administrative confusion, and service complaints that trigger total relationship liquidation.

### Presenter Notes
> "This slide challenges decades of traditional banking dogma. We have always pushed frontline staff to cross-sell as many products as possible. But look at the data: two products is our golden sweet spot. Beyond two, churn spikes to 83%, and every single customer with four products left the bank. Aggressive cross-selling is destroying our customer relationships."

## Slide 7: Engagement Signals — The Activity Delta

### Visual Layout
- Side-by-side comparative cards:
  - Active Members: **14.27%** Churn
  - Inactive Members: **26.85%** Churn
  - Engagement Multiplier: **+88.2% Risk Surge**

### Slide Content
- **The Leading Indicator:** Account inactivity is the primary behavioral precursor to formal account closure.
- **The Silent Transition:** Customers do not abruptly close accounts; they incrementally divert direct deposits and recurring bills to competitor banks 3 to 6 months prior to exiting.
- **The Opportunity Window:** Catching inactivity early provides a reliable window for automated re-engagement.

### Presenter Notes
> "Inactivity is our early-warning smoke detector. Inactive accounts churn at nearly 27%, compared to 14% for active users. When a customer stops using their card or app, they are already halfway out the door."

## Slide 11: Strategic Mitigation Pillar 1 — German Market Overhaul

### Visual Layout
- 3 Action Pillars for the German Operating Subsidiary.

### Slide Content
- **Market Intervention:**
  1. *Tiered Yield Matching:* Introduce competitive yield tiers on savings balances above €50,000 to eliminate rate shopping.
  2. *Fee Restructuring:* Abolish flat account maintenance charges on high-balance accounts; replace with relationship-based loyalty waivers.
  3. *Automated Money Market Sweeps:* Partner with European liquidity funds to automatically sweep idle checking funds into yield-bearing reserves.

### Presenter Notes
> "In Germany, our immediate priority is fee restructuring and yield parity. By introducing automated yield sweeps on balances over €50,000, we remove the primary incentive for German customers to migrate deposits to fintechs or cooperative banks."

## Slide 14: Strategic Mitigation Pillar 4 — Automated Early-Warning System (EWS)

### Visual Layout
- Automated Triggers & Playbook Workflow:
  - 60-Day Inactivity Detected $\rightarrow$ Risk Score Calculated $\rightarrow$ Automated Playbook Dispatched.

### Slide Content
- **Real-Time Automated Surveillance:**
  - System monitors 60-day rolling transaction volume and direct debit frequency.
  - Accounts exhibiting a $>50\%$ decline in monthly velocity are flagged as `At-Risk Dormant`.
- **Segment-Differentiated Response:**
  - *Standard Accounts:* Automated promotional cashback or zero-fee period activation.
  - *High-Value Accounts ($> €50k$):* Mandatory 48-hour outreach call from a Senior Relationship Manager.

### Presenter Notes
> "Our fourth pillar is technology-driven: an automated Early-Warning System. When an account exhibits 60 days of inactivity, the system immediately flags the risk. For affluent accounts, this triggers a personal phone call from an advisor within 48 hours."
